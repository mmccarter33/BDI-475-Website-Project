Sale price medians include sales of $1,000 or more, excluding the impossible low values noted in the data description. Days on market is contract date minus list date, calculated only for sales with both dates; blank dates are not treated as zero. Averages can be pulled up by long marketing periods, and listed sales are not a random sample of all sales.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026), collected March 2026.
